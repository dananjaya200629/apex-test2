APEX HOLDINGS OMS — V24
- WhatsApp Bot, Messages page, bot menu items, dashboard "WhatsApp Bot Reports" card and Settings "WhatsApp Connect (Bot)" are NOT in this version.
- Lead List: pick Start/End date (or Last 7 days / Last 30 days / All dates) to view older leads of every status. Swapped dates are corrected automatically.
- Lead import (and Reassign Selected): the Company Admin's own account can now be chosen as an assignee, next to Sales and Shipping users.
- Dashboard: the company toggles below "All Company Comparison" are a compact grid inside the card; nothing is cropped or overlapped (desktop + mobile).
- Stock Request: the Company Admin must attach the payment receipt (image or PDF) before "Send Stock Request" works. Images are compressed automatically; PDFs up to 1.5 MB.
  The Owner sees a "Receipt" column in Company Stock Requests (View / Open in new tab / Download). A company can open only its own receipts and sees them in My Stock Purchases / Requests.
  Older requests created before this update show "No receipt".
  Data is still stored in the browser (localStorage), so very many receipts can fill it; clear old stock requests if a "storage is full" message appears.
- Company List (Owner): Edit (company name, admin username, password, manager, address, phone), Block / Unblock and Delete buttons are back on every company row. A blocked company cannot log in.
- Stock List: search bar for everyone (name, product code, price, stock qty; commas in prices are ignored). Owner's Stock List also has a Company column and a Company filter showing which company each product belongs to.
- Font: the whole system uses the Inter font, bundled inside assets/fonts (works offline, license in assets/fonts/Inter-OFL-LICENSE.txt). Sinhala/Tamil text uses the device's Sinhala/Tamil fonts.
