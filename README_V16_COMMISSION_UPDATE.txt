APEX HOLDINGS OMS v11 — Commission & Search Update

This build is based on the existing standalone v11 OMS and adds only the latest requested commission/search controls.

1. Staff commissions
- Company Admin can configure each non-admin staff member separately.
- Commission method per staff: Orders only / Delivered only / Both.
- Order rate and Delivered rate can be different for every staff member.
- Current-month commission is recalculated automatically.

2. Company commissions
- Company commission is earned from approved stock purchases made by child companies created under that Admin company.
- It is not calculated from the parent/Admin company's own purchases.
- Owner can change each Admin company's company-commission rate live.
- Default rate is 6%.
- Company Admin sees January-December monthly commission cards for their own company.
- Owner sees all companies with Company / Month / Status filters.
- Paid button is locked until the selected month has ended.
- Once paid, status becomes Paid.

3. Search Orders
- All historical orders are retained.
- Date range, Staff, Status and keyword search remain available.
- A per-order Commission column is shown.
- Matching order quantity and commission totals are shown at the bottom.
- Sales/Shipping staff see only their own orders; Admin sees all company staff orders; Owner sees all orders.
