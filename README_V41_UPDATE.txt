APEX HOLDINGS OMS V41 — STAFF DASHBOARD & FINANCE UPDATE

- Staff (Sales / Shipping / Staff roles) dashboard now shows only:
  All Company Comparison, TOTAL ORDERS, PENDING ORDERS (Waiting to ship),
  SHIPPED, RETURNED, DELIVERED (Completed orders), and SALES.
- Removed the additional personal-performance cards, donut charts, and dashboard
  action panel from the staff dashboard. Admin and Owner dashboards remain intact.
- Profit and Net Profit are now separate values:
  Profit = recognised delivered Sales minus product-master costs.
  Expenses = delivery and other order expenses (returned orders retain relevant
  delivery/master charges).
  Net Profit = Profit minus Expenses.
- Added a separate Net Profit series/toggle to Sales, Profit & Expense Analytics.
- Analytics chart Y axes now display rupee values (Rs.) and sale quantity (PCS)
  instead of percentage-only axis labels.
- Master Delivery Charge is editable inline from the Order Financial Details table;
  the edited value is saved to the order and used by financial calculations.
- Removed the standalone Delivery Charge column from the Order Financial Details
  table. Other Cost remains visible and editable.
- Updated report KPIs and table to show Profit and Net Profit separately.
- Existing order records and other OMS modules are preserved.
