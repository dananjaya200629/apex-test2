APEX HOLDINGS OMS V42 — FINANCIAL TABLE & RETURN EXPENSE REFINEMENTS

- Returned-order expenses now include only Master Delivery Charge, Other Expense
  (product-master other expense), and Other Cost (order-level other expense).
  Standard delivery fee is not added to a returned order expense total.
- The Order Financial Details table groups Sales, Total Expenses, Profit, and
  Net Profit together for quick reading.
- Total Expenses and Net Profit use a red style; Profit remains green.
- The Total Expenses column displays the full expense total for each order.
- The footer totals row shows totals only for Sales, Total Expenses, Profit, and
  Net Profit; component expense columns and Sale Price have no footer totals.
- The Sales, Profit & Expense Analytics item has been removed from the sidebar menu.
  The report route remains available to existing internal navigation links.
- Existing order and company data structures are preserved.
