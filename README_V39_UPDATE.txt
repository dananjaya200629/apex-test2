APEX HOLDINGS OMS — V39 RETURN ACCOUNTING & COST ANALYTICS UPDATE

- Return accounting: a Returned order keeps its original sale value in Sales; Wholesale Cost and Company Stock Price are excluded from returned expenses. Only delivery-related and other costs are counted. Profit is fixed at Rs. 0 for Returned orders.
- Master Details pack costing: Real Cost = (Wholesale Price × PCS) + Company Stock Price + Inbound Delivery Charge + Other Expense. The last three values are added once per pack and do not multiply with PCS.
- New orders save a cost breakdown snapshot for Wholesale, Company Stock, Inbound Delivery and Other Expense.
- Sales, Profit and Expenses dashboard chart uses one Sri Lankan Rupee axis, daily dates on the horizontal axis and smooth curved lines. Sale Quantity (PCS) is removed from the chart only.
- Sales are recognised for Shipped, Delivered and Returned orders; a status change does not change the order sale price.
- Sales & Profit Report displays each cost component, order delivery cost, order other cost, total expenses and profit. Expense columns use a light red highlight; Profit uses a light green highlight.
- Previous OMS workflows, user roles, stock controls, shipping and waybill layouts are retained.
